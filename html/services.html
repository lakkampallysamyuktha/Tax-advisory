Individual Taxation

Personal filings, kept personal.

Individual work covers more ground than a single return. The tabs below show the four engagement types the desk takes on.

Annual return, prepared to be re-read.

Return preparation starts with the source documents, not the prior year's return. The prior year is used as a reconciliation baseline, never as a template. Where the two disagree, we find out why before filing.

  • ScopeIncome computation, schedule preparation, filing
  • InputsBank statements, Form 26AS, AIS, investment proofs
  • DeliverableFiled set plus a short reconciliation note
  • TimingBegins six to eight weeks before the applicable due date

Capital gains, traced to the transaction.

Gains work is transactional. Every entry in the computation traces back to a contract note, a bank credit or a registration record. Where the cost is inherited or historical, the basis of the estimate is described in writing.

  • ScopeShort and long term gains, exemptions, set-off
  • InputsContract notes, purchase records, registration documents
  • DeliverableComputation with source reference on each line
  • TimingBefore the transaction is completed where possible

NRI and cross-border, with the treaty position stated.

Residential status, treaty eligibility and withholding on payments are treated as one question, not three. The treaty position is stated in writing, along with the documents required to support it.

  • ScopeResidential status, DTAA position, withholding, repatriation
  • InputsPassport and travel history, foreign income documents, TRC
  • DeliverablePosition note plus the required forms
  • TimingPer transaction, not annual

Notice response, built from the original file.

A notice is not answered by writing a new submission. It is answered by going back to the file that produced the position in the first place, and re-presenting it in the form the officer has requested.

  • ScopeReply drafting, submission, representation up to appeal
  • InputsOriginal working papers, prior filings, notice copy
  • DeliverableWritten submission plus indexed evidence bundle
  • TimingWithin the response window, tracked by name

Corporate & Business Tax

Business positions, held steady.

Corporate work expands the register. The panels below show the four engagement types the business desk takes on.

Preparation begins from the audited or management accounts, with every adjustment tied to a ledger reference. Positions taken in the return are mirrored in a written note, and the note travels with the working papers.

  • Computation and return preparation
  • Advance tax calibration through the year
  • Reconciliation to books and prior filings
  • Position note per material adjustment
  • Source index on the entire working set
  • Signed review memo before submission

Assessment work is a continuation of the original file, not a new argument. Submissions are drafted from the same source index used at filing, so that the position presented to the officer matches the position filed.

  • Reply drafting and submission
  • Evidence bundle, indexed to the submission
  • Representation at first and second appeal
  • Alignment with the original position note
  • Named advisor owning each notice
  • Dated register of open notices

Compliance is delivered as a register, not as a reminder service. Every obligation has a named owner, a date and a status. Items moving out of tolerance are escalated before the deadline is at risk, not after it is missed.

  • Withholding tax deposit cycle
  • Indirect tax return cycle
  • ROC and secretarial filings
  • Dated register with named owners
  • Monthly status review
  • Exception log reviewed at practice level

Cross-border work begins with the treaty position and works outward. Benchmarking studies, local files and entity-level reporting are assembled so that the study and the ledger tell the same story.

  • Transfer pricing study and local file
  • Country-by-country reporting support
  • Treaty position for withholding on payments
  • Study reconciled to the ledger
  • Documentation assembled before the reporting date
  • Written position on each intercompany stream

Audit & Assurance

Where the paper trail meets the file.

Audit work is judged on how the file reads a year later. The comparison below sets out what the practice commits to, and what that commitment looks like in practice.

What we commit to

A working paper standard that survives a cold read.

  • Every figure traces to a source document
  • Every working paper carries a preparer's mark
  • Every file is read by an advisor from another desk
  • Every material conclusion has a written reasoning note
  • Every file is retained beyond the applicable statutory window
How it reads in the file

What a reviewer sees, without asking.

  • A source index at the front of the file
  • Position notes beside the adjustments they support
  • A signed review memo before sign-off
  • A reconciliation narrative a reader can follow cold
  • A named advisor accountable for the file

Planning & Structuring

Design before the transaction, not after.

Structures are chosen once and lived with for years. The practice treats structuring as a documentary exercise: what the structure will look like on entry, through operation, and at exit.

Every recommendation is tested against three questions. What does the structure look like under current law? What happens if the applicable rate or provision changes? And what does the file look like if the structure is ever examined?

Engagement Models

Three ways we hold a file.

Engagement terms follow the shape of the work rather than a fixed template. The three models below cover most arrangements.

Standing compliance

A retained desk for the recurring filing cycle. The register is kept by the practice, and every obligation is owned by name.

  • Monthly and quarterly cycle
  • Named advisor assigned
  • Status register shared with the client

Project engagement

A defined scope with a fixed deliverable — a restatement, a transaction review, a documentation exercise.

  • Written scope before work begins
  • Fixed deliverable and date
  • Working papers handed over at close

Advisory retainer

An on-call advisory relationship for questions that arise between filings, without a separate engagement per question.

  • Standing availability
  • Written advice per question
  • Quarterly review of open positions
